The question

Let $A$ be a representation-finite finite-dimensional algebra. Count quotient-closed and submodule-closed additive subcategories by the number of indecomposable isomorphism classes they contain. If $q_i(A)$ and $s_i(A)$ are those two counts at level $i$, the strong OP conjecture asks whether

$$Q_A(t)=\sum_i q_i(A)t^i \quad = \quad \sum_i s_i(A)t^i=S_A(t).$$

The formalization models right $A$-modules as finitely generated modules over $A^{\mathrm{op}}$ and encodes the two families as closure systems on a canonical finite skeleton of indecomposable isomorphism classes.

What Lean proves here

Finite boundary

At most nine indecomposables

Equality of the first and last four coefficients covers every possible degree.

Serial structure

Nakayama algebras

The two level polynomials are identified through products of chain polynomials.

Separated model

Radical square zero

A classification-free separated-algebra and representation-directed route proves equidistribution.

AR combinatorics

Representation-directed

A canonical reduced Auslander–Reiten word gives matching quotient and submodule profiles.
